Before you start

DedSec CCTV shows public camera streams that are already available on the open internet. It is not a tool for accessing private or secured cameras.

You need a DedSec Lab account to sign in. Create one from the app's sign-in screen or through the community Discord if you do not have access yet.

A VPN is recommended while you browse public streams. Your own network still sees the traffic unless you use one.

By continuing you agree to use the software only for lawful purposes and only with streams you are allowed to view.
